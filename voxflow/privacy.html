Privacy Policy — Preview

VoxFlow stores account information needed to operate the service, including name, email, login sessions, channel settings and connected-platform identifiers.

Security

Passwords are stored as cryptographic hashes. Platform connections use OAuth so VoxFlow does not ask users for their social-network passwords. Production credentials and tokens are intended to remain server-side.

Data control

Users can request or perform account deletion. Before public commercial launch, this preview policy will be replaced with a complete LGPD-compliant policy including retention, legal basis, subprocessors and contact details.
