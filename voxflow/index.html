RECOMMENDED START

Google in. YouTube out.

The cleanest first setup is simple: use Google for account access, then grant YouTube publishing permission separately only when you want VoxFlow to publish. Login access and publishing access stay separated.

①

Sign in with Google

Fast account access without creating another password, while keeping your VoxFlow profile tied to a verified Google identity.

②

Connect YouTube Shorts

Authorize uploads separately. VoxFlow never needs your YouTube password and stores platform tokens encrypted on the server.

③

Add TikTok & Reels next

Start with one stable publishing path, then expand the same content operation to TikTok and Instagram as their app connections are approved.
