WildVox

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

WildVox is a creator tool used to prepare, review, and publish educational wildlife videos to social media accounts that the creator explicitly authorizes.

Information we process

When a creator connects a supported social account, WildVox may process authorization tokens, account or creator identifiers, profile information made available through the platform API, video files selected for publishing, captions, publishing settings, and technical publishing status or error information.

How we use information

We use this information only to authenticate the authorized account, prepare and publish content requested by the creator, verify publishing status, troubleshoot failures, prevent duplicate publishing, and maintain the security and reliability of the service.

TikTok integration

When TikTok is connected, WildVox uses TikTok's official APIs and OAuth authorization. WildVox does not request or store a user's TikTok password. TikTok data is used only for functions the creator has authorized, including creator information queries and content publishing where approved.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to TikTok or other service providers only as needed to provide the requested publishing functionality, hosting, storage, security, or operational support.

Data retention

Authorization credentials are retained only as long as needed to keep the integration active or until they expire or are revoked. Operational logs and publishing metadata may be retained for a limited period for reliability, security, and troubleshooting.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect access credentials and publishing data. Sensitive credentials are kept in server-side secret storage and are not intentionally exposed in public source code or public pages.

Your choices

Creators may revoke platform access through the relevant platform account settings. Revoking authorization may prevent WildVox from publishing or accessing account information. Requests related to WildVox data can be submitted through the project's support channel.

Children

WildVox is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through its creator publishing integration.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service or applicable platform requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material revisions are published.

Contact

For privacy or support requests, use the WildVox project support channel at GitHub Issues.
